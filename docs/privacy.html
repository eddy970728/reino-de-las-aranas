Privacy policy

Spider Kingdom: Awakening · LunaDoggyBru Studios · Last updated: November 27, 2026

Spider Kingdom: Awakening is a game by LunaDog Games, the video game division of LunaDoggyBru Studios. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

We do not collect data

The game does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, ads, in-app purchases, analytics or third-party trackers. It works offline.

Your saves

Your progress (saves, achievements and settings) is stored only on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android itself may copy that data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game. That backup is handled by Google under your settings and its privacy policy; we have no access to it.

If you use the export option, the game shows you a text code. That code stays with you: you decide where to keep it.

Email contact

If you choose “Contact the creator”, your email app opens. We only receive what you choose to send, and we use it only to reply to you and improve the game.

Children

The game is intended for ages 13 and up and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its date.
